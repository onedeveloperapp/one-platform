# ONE's browser extension: filing in the lawyer's own session

The extension is the second of the three ways to file the owner chose on 2026-11-20 (root `CLAUDE.md`, "Three ways to file"): ONE files from its own browser with the lawyer's court login, which the lawyer hands over; **a lawyer who keeps the login to themself files through this extension, on the session they opened with their own login, password and second factor**; or the lawyer files by hand with ONE's package and guide. In the lawyer's browser nothing of the login passes through ONE: the lawyer enters, and the extension does the typing. The courts ask a second factor at every login (Projudi: a code by e-mail; eproc of TJSP and TJRJ: an authenticator app), and each court writes that the credential is the lawyer's own (below).

It lives inside `apps/web` as the clock does (`apps/web/clock/`): its logic is the app's code (`src/lib/court-extension/`, tested by vitest in jsdom), its glue is here, and `build.mjs` bundles it with esbuild.

## Version 0.2: filing a batch

The lawyer chose, in ONE, to file at a system through the extension, and approved there a batch of ready packages of lawsuits they sign (`/protocolo`, with the second factor). In Chrome, the toolbar's button opens the extension's panel beside the court's screen (`panel.html`, `src/panel.ts`), where it stays while ONE files.

- **Linking, once** (`Ligar ao ONE`): the lawyer types the code of seven letters ONE shows them where they chose the extension (their setup's step "Como você protocola", or "Protocolo nos tribunais" in their settings), valid ten minutes. ONE answers a token the extension keeps in the browser (`chrome.storage.local`), kept as its SHA-256 alone on ONE's side (`extension_links`, 0263); unlinking in ONE ends it. Nothing of the court's login, password or code ever passes through the extension's calls.
- **The batches** (`Protocolar`): the panel lists the batches the lawyer approved for their extension, with how many lawsuits each holds and whether ONE still learns the system. A batch starts on the court's tab in front, where the lawyer is logged in with their own login and second factor; a tab of another system is refused with what to open.
- **The filing** (`src/lib/court-extension/batch-run.ts`, over the runner of `src/lib/court-robot/runner.ts`, the same ONE's own browser runs): lawsuit by lawsuit, ONE reads each screen as structure (version 0.1's capture with the actions of a table's rows, never a value nor a person), follows the system's map or asks its AI through ONE (`protocolo.tela`: the facts by key, the values typed by the extension alone), checks the plan, and acts on the court's tab through the content script (`src/driver.ts` over `src/lib/court-extension/act.ts`: a value set as typing sets it, with the events typing fires; a line of the system's own list; a box ticked by a click; a button pressed; a file of the package attached; never a password, a hidden or disabled field, a court's dialog). The package's files come from links ONE hands for fifteen minutes.
- **Before sending, while ONE learns the system**: the filing is held at the first button that may send ("Finalizar" counts: in the eproc of TJSP it opens the summary, and elsewhere it may send at once), and the panel asks the lawyer's word with the court's screen beside it ("Protocolar esta ação" or "Parar o lote"). The word may also come from ONE (`/protocolo`), or from the lawyer's own press on the court's screen: then what they pressed may have sent the lawsuit, the filing is marked as going, and ONE presses nothing for them, going on only to the court's summary or receipt. The database marks a filing as going right before the press that may send it (`filing_submission_sending`) and refuses one ONE learns with no lawyer's word.
- **What only the lawyer can do** pauses the batch, with what to do on the court's screen and "Continuar" or "Parar o lote": the court asks the login again, asks its code, shows a term or a message, refuses a datum, or ONE cannot go on in a screen (the lawyer may do that step themself). Nothing of it is done for them.
- **What comes back to ONE**: the number the court's receipt shows, read by the extension's code, and the court's screen as read, kept as the receipt, a PDF of the firm (`receiptBlocks`). A filing whose answer cannot be read once it may have gone is unknown, stops the batch, and the panel says where to look before anything of it goes again. The path ONE learned reading screens is sent as the platform's map of the system, checked first (`readSteps`), and kept as one ONE still learns: a path learned in a lawyer's browser is never trusted by it, since every firm would follow a trusted map with no AI and no lawyer's word; ONE's own browser makes a map trusted.
- **Stopping**: "Parar o lote" in the panel, or in ONE. What never went is let go, each package free for another batch; what went, or may have gone, stays as it is. Closing the panel in the middle stops the run: the batch is then only stopped (in ONE) and approved again.

ONE's side is `/api/extensao/v1/...` (`apps/web/src/app/api/extensao/v1/[...rota]/route.ts`, `lib/filing-ways/extension-api.ts`): every call but linking carries the token, and each batch and filing is found as that lawyer's, of that firm, filed by their extension, before any function of the database (0263, 0264, 0267) is called. What the extension sends is checked before it reaches the AI or the platform's map (`lib/filing-ways/extension-protocol.ts`), and the AI reads at most sixty screens of one filing.

## The diagnosis of version 0.1: mapping the screens

Under "Mapear as telas do tribunal", the panel keeps version 0.1's mapping, a diagnosis for when a screen surprises ONE, never a step of the filing. The extension talks to no server for it: the map is a file the lawyer downloads.

- **Map this screen** (`Mapear esta tela`): the structure of the page in every frame (the Projudi draws some screens in frames): each field with its label, type, id, name and a selector to find it again; each select's own list (codes and names); each button and each step link; each table's column names and its number of rows; the headings; the parameters of the address that name the screen (`acao`, `PaginaAtual`).
- **Record the path** (`Gravar o caminho`): each click on a button, a link, a checkbox or a file field, and each change, in order: which field, the code chosen from a system's own list, whether a box was left ticked. A text field says only that it was filled.
- **Save the map** (`Baixar o mapa`): a JSON file.

What is never kept, as code (`capture.ts`, and its tests): a field's value; a hidden field (they carry the session's tokens); a list of people (a select whose label or name speaks of a party, a lawyer, a user: only its size); a row of a table; a link inside a table or that names a file; a hash, a token or a number in the address; and, in any text kept, an e-mail or a run of ten digits or more (a CPF, a CNPJ, a lawsuit's number).

### Build, load, check

1. `node apps/web/extension/build.mjs` writes `apps/web/extension/dist/` (git-ignored), calling ONE at its production address; `--one <address>` builds it for another (a preview, or `http://localhost:3000` on a development machine), and `--out <folder>` writes elsewhere. ONE's address goes into the manifest's hosts.
2. In Chrome, `chrome://extensions`, turn on the developer mode, "Carregar sem compactação", and choose `dist/`. To send it to a lawyer, zip what `dist/` holds (`Compress-Archive apps/web/extension/dist/* one-extensao.zip`). It is not in the Chrome Web Store yet.
3. `node apps/web/extension/smoke.mjs` builds its own copy calling a stand-in of ONE that the browser serves (no request reaches ONE or a court), loads it in Playwright's Chromium, and checks, against stand-ins of four screens of the eproc of TJSP: the link by code, the batch listed, the value of the cause typed, nothing posted to the court before the lawyer's word, the petition posted after it, the summary confirmed, the number and the receipt brought back, the path kept with no value, person or token; then version 0.1's diagnosis at a stand-in of the eproc of TJRJ with a frame (both frames mapped, the path recorded without what was typed, nothing of a value, a token or a person in the file, nothing recorded once stopped).

The hosts it may touch are only the systems it knows and ONE: `manifest.json` and `COURT_SYSTEMS` in `capture.ts` say the same courts (a test checks it), and the build adds ONE's address. Its permissions are `storage` (the link, and the map of the diagnosis), `scripting` (reading and acting on the court's frames) and `sidePanel`.

## The systems

- **eproc of TJSP** (`eproc1g.tjsp.jus.br`, joined on 2026-11-20, the first one a lawyer of the pilot maps): its small-claims and civil courts take new lawsuits there (`court_systems`, migration 0021, from the DJEN of 2026-10-08). The lawyer enters with login and password or a certificate, both with the six digits of an authenticator app ("conta eproc TJSP"), and may tick "Não usar o 2FA neste dispositivo e navegador" on a device of their own (TJSP, "Primeiros passos no sistema eproc para advogado", 17/07/2025; ONE's own browser never ticks it). From TJSP's manual "Da distribuição de inicial" (13/04/2026): "Petição Inicial" in the menu or on the "Painel do Advogado" opens "Peticionamento Eletrônico" in five steps: (1) Informações do processo (the city, "Valor da causa", "Rito", "Área", "Classe processual", "Nível de Sigilo do Processo", "+Incluir outros advogados" by "Login/OAB"; in the rite "JUIZADO ESPECIAL", "Renuncia valor excedente a 60 salários mínimos"), (2) Assuntos ("Filtrar", "Assuntos selecionados", "Incluir"), (3) the plaintiffs and (4) the defendants ("Tipo Pessoa", CPF or CNPJ, "Consultar", which reads the Receita Federal, "Incluir"; for each party "Principal?", "Tipo Representação", "Justiça Gratuita" ("Requerida") and, obligatorily, "Adicionar Endereço e Contato" with an e-mail and a mobile, or "Declaro que desconheço o endereço da parte" for a defendant), (5) Documentos (the file, its "Tipo" and its sigilo, the priorities and requests, "Confirmar seleção de documento"), then "Finalizar", a summary and "Confirmar ajuizamento", which gives the number, the "chave para consulta", the class, the judge and the court. A lawsuit may instead be saved "para distribuição futura" (it waits in "Processos pendentes do advogado"), which lets a lawyer map every step without filing. PDFs go plain: the login signs (Resolução 963/2025, art. 19, sole paragraph).
- **Projudi of TJGO** (`projudi.tjgo.jus.br`): the lawyer enters with CPF and password and a code of six characters sent to their e-mail (valid five minutes), with the PDPJ, or with a certificate. Its screens for a new lawsuit are not in any manual found: the first map is the pilot firm's filing of 2026-11-20. The files go already signed (`.p7s`), which ONE does with the lawyer's cloud signature (`package-signature-work.ts`).
- **eproc of TJRJ** (`eproc1g.tjrj.jus.br`): login, password and an authenticator app's code, with "Não usar o 2FA neste dispositivo e navegador" for a trusted device. From TJRJ's own manual for lawyers (v1.0): the menu "Petição inicial", then "Peticionamento Eletrônico" in five steps: (1) Informações do processo ("Desejo entrar com a ação em", "Rito", "Área", "Classe processual", "Nível de Sigilo do Processo", "Valor da Causa" or "Não se aplica", "+ Incluir outros advogados" by login or OAB), (2) Assuntos (a tree, "Assuntos Selecionados", "Incluir"), (3) Partes autores and (4) Partes réus (a search by "Tipo Pessoa", CPF or CNPJ, "Consultar", "Incluir"; a person the court does not know is registered in "Cadastro de Pessoa Física" with address and contacts), (5) Documentos ("Informações de Custas" with the GRERJ or the "Motivo para não recolhimento", "Informações Adicionais" with the priorities and "Opção por Juízo 100% Digital", "Conciliação", one "Escolher arquivo" per document with its "Tipo" and "Sigilo", "Confirmar seleção de documentos"), and then "Finalizar", which gives the receipt, or "Salvar para Distribuição Futura". eproc takes plain PDFs: the act is the login's. The civil courts are on eproc; TJRJ's small-claims courts move to it by groups (Aviso Conjunto TJ/CGJ 38/2026: some units of the Capital on 2026-10-29, others on 2027-01-22), the rest staying on the legacy DCP until then, where the filing stays assisted.

## The courts' written rules (checked before a screen is automated)

- **The CNJ, for every court:** "O Conselho Nacional de Justiça, o Conselho da Justiça Federal, o Conselho Superior da Justiça do Trabalho e os tribunais a eles vinculados poderão limitar ou bloquear o acesso a sistemas ou serviços, via API ou não, caso identificado comportamento inautêntico ou consumo abusivo de dados por parte do usuário", inauthentic being, among others, "utilização de rotinas automatizadas que simulem o comportamento humano" and "uso de técnicas ou soluções projetadas para burlar regras e restrições do sistema" (Portaria Presidência 316/2024, art. 9, § 1, II and V; the act's object is the CNJ's datalake, but this article speaks of any system). It does not forbid; it lets the court block the lawyer's access. Its diagnosis reads the page when the lawyer clicks and sends the court nothing. Version 0.2 files: it fills and sends on the court's screen at the pace the court's system takes, never disguised, never working around a court's restriction (no captcha solved, no limit dodged).
- **TJSP** (Resolução 963/2025 of the Órgão Especial, read whole on 2026-11-20 from TJSP's public API of legislation): the user must "resguardar o sigilo de suas credenciais de acesso" (art. 18, I) and "abster-se de práticas que causem sobrecarga ao sistema, incluindo o uso não autorizado, por usuários externos, de robôs de consulta" (art. 18, V); "uso inadequado" is "toda atividade que evidencie ataque cibernético, violação de dados ou uso desproporcional dos ativos computacionais" (art. 37), answered by "bloqueio preventivo do usuário, temporário e total" (art. 37, sole paragraph), and a blocked lawyer's case goes to the OAB's section (art. 38, sole paragraph). The MNI is for "Órgãos e instituições públicas" (art. 22). Nothing in it speaks of robots that file. The owner accepted on 2026-11-20 the risk of the way of filing with the lawyer's login kept by ONE there too (art. 18, I). Not read: the Provimento Conjunto 326/2025 on registering external users (only its arts. 25 to 28 were at hand) and the Provimento CSM 2.834/2026, which by its summaries rules eproc's steering committee.
- **TJGO:** the credentials are personal and non-transferable (Resolução 59/2016, art. 11), which the extension never touches; no rule of the TJGO's own against automation was found, and the tribunal blocks by behaviour.
- **TJRJ:** the credentials are personal and non-transferable (Ato Normativo TJ 9/2010, for the court's systems, and the security page of the portal); no written rule on robots was found. The act that rules eproc's use and governance, **Aviso Normativo Conjunto TJ/CGJ 19/2026**, sits behind a check of the TJRJ's site that a script cannot pass and was not read: it is read before the TJRJ filing is turned on.

## Known limits, and next

- No real court's screen was filed yet: the first is the eproc of TJSP with the pilot's lawyer, whose first filing stops before "Finalizar" for their word.
- The receipt kept is the court's screen as read; the court's own receipt stays in its system.
- A court's dialog (a box asking to confirm) waits for the lawyer to answer it on the screen.
- A batch whose panel was closed in the middle is only stopped, then approved again.
- ONE's own browser on ONE's server (the first way, 2026-11-23, `apps/web/clock/navegador.ts`) runs the same filling over the same maps with the lawyer's court login, checks a login as the lawyer gives it, and makes a map trusted.
