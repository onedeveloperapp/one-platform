# Product (authenticated app) UX

- The `product-screen` skill is MANDATORY for any create/edit here — a `PreToolUse` hook (`.claude/hooks/product-screen-guard.mjs`) loads it automatically; you never wait to be asked.
- **Goal, not table**: design from the user's job and what success looks like. Never default to a CRUD table of rows — that shape is allowed only when the job really is scan/compare/manage many rows.
- **Never a blank screen**: every zero-data view uses `EmptyState` (icon + why + the ONE next action). A brand-new user must find a path to value (the aha moment).
- **Less text on the screens, and no empty space** (the owner, 2026-11-09): a page, a card or a section opens on its title, and what it is for sits behind the "i" beside it (`description` of `PageHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CollapsibleCard` and `SectionTitle`; 200 characters at most, kept by `lib/i18n/hint-budget.test.ts`). `detail` keeps in sight only state or data (the kind of a case, a conversation's number, how many a list shows) and what must be read before acting (leaving the firm, what each decision of `/settings/autonomia` does, what a lawyer authorizes with the cloud signature, the release of a real passenger's alerts, and the instructions on the client's and the passenger's own pages). A row or a column that opens a list shows a chevron, never a sentence saying so; a definition that repeats its title goes; a tile is its icon and its name. A section with nothing in it takes a drawing of `@/icons/illustrations` (`EmptyState` `illustration`): the court with no lawsuit, the board with no case going, the goals with none, a module with no case, and every list with nothing in it (the cases, the conversations, the publications, the deadlines, the agenda, the filing queue, the library, the approvals, the operator's consoles); a state of permission or of a failed read keeps its icon. The owner found the controladoria, a module's area and the library tiring to read: 136 blocks of text under titles, about 2,800 words. The case screen had the same, card by card (2026-11-10): each card with nothing in it takes its drawing beside its words (the dossier's, in the narrow column, above them), a section inside a card says what it is behind the "i" of its sub-heading (`SectionTitle` `variant="subtitle2"` `as="h3"`: the suspension of the Tema 1.417, the acceptance of the civil court, the audios of the case, the preview of a piece), the way out of ONE's automation (a petition written outside, the reason a family goes together) sits behind an `InfoHint` beside its state line, and a state line drops the sentence that repeats the button beside it.
- **Onboarding never diverts attention**: a setup or onboarding screen (`/configurar`, the setup on the home, any `SetupWizard`) carries nothing that leads away from finishing it: no link to another screen, no "see more", no list of things to explore. Each step does its work in place (dialogs, inline forms, the second factor in the step); what is information for later lives on the home or its own page. A line of modules with "Ver módulos" on the first step of `/configurar` took the owner out of a setup half done (2026-10-30).
- **Prerequisites come first**: in a setup or any multi-step flow, whatever a later step cannot be done without (the second factor, a role, a connection, a fact the database checks) is its own step before the first step that needs it, and the flow does not advance past it until it is met. Never let a person reach a step and find it locked by something an earlier step could have settled. The second factor is the first step of `/configurar` for this reason (2026-11-03): it was asked inside six steps, and blocked the owner at the first.
- **ONE does the work, a person answers** (the owner, 2026-11-03): before a screen has a person do a task by hand, ask whether ONE's AI can do it from what the firm already has (read a document, fill a record from it, make a model of it, check it against a rule, ask for what is missing), and build that instead. The screen shows what ONE did and that ONE may err, and asks only what only the person knows or decides; a document ONE cannot use is refused with why and the way to send another. How much ONE does alone is each firm's (`autonomy_decisions`, ONE by default). The setup's step of the papers had no way to send the firm's own power of attorney: the firm sends the document it already uses, and ONE makes the model (migration 0079). The model keeps the firm's words (0093): only what changes per case becomes a mark, each field of the firm's form its own (`[[NOME]]`, `[[CPF]]`, `[[ENDERECO]]`), and the firm's society and lawyers stay as written once it says they are its own; the case says when a lawyer who signs is not among them.
- **ONE's work is never the person's task** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a checklist, a count, a pending line, an empty state or a message names as the person's only what a person does or decides. What ONE does by itself (opens a case from a module, sends a link, creates the lawsuits, writes a piece) is said as ONE's work, done, being done or waited for ("O ONE espera o primeiro caso"), never as a step the person owes, and a manual way to do it is offered as the exception it is, never as the primary action. "Abrir o primeiro caso" on the firm's setup list made a firm that had just finished its setup feel the work was manual, the opposite of what ONE is sold on.
- **A registration starts from documents** (the owner, 2026-11-03): where the record is already written on a document the person has (an identity, a proof of address, a ticket, a booking's screenshot, a photo of a printed page), the form opens on sending it; ONE reads it, fills the fields and marks each as read, and the person confirms or corrects what ONE read, being asked by hand only what no document gives. Typing every field is the contingency, offered as "Preencher à mão", never the first screen. The agency's new passenger was a form of typed fields while ONE already read the identity, the proof of address and the ticket on the passenger's page; it now starts from them (migration 0083).
- **A card asks only for what is still owed** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a request already fulfilled (a document that arrived and serves, a link already sent, an answer given) leaves the card's main area. The card says it as done (`StateLine` `done`, a green check; when nothing is left in the card, one line on its tint that says so: "Não precisa enviar de novo") and keeps doing it again one click away, in `QuickActions`. What is still owed, or arrived and does not serve, is its own line in the attention colour with its one button beside it. The passenger's link showed "Enviar identidade" to a passenger whose agency had already sent it.
- **Where things stand is said in icon and colour**: every card a person works through leads with the icon of what it is (`CardTitle`, its family's tint) and says where it stands in a `StatusChip`; each thing listed in it says its state in a `StateLine`: done in green with a check, under way in amber with a clock, to look at or act on in accent-3 with an exclamation, a failure in red (kept for failures), information in grey. The words always say the state too, never the colour alone.
- **An act the person must understand is confirmed in front of them**: what binds them or what others will act on (an agreement given or taken back, a decision) is confirmed in an `OutcomeDialog`: the state in a large icon, the title in their words ("Acordo registrado"), each consequence on its own line with its icon, one button ("Entendi"). A line of `Alert` at the top of the page is read past: the passenger who agreed on their link could not tell what they had agreed to start.
- **A check can be given, and a wait on the client has a way forward** (2026-11-04, migration 0094): what ONE read of a person waits for a look with "Conferi com o documento" beside it, and saving the person counts as that look (`confirm_person_read_fields`); a case whose account still lacks the client's answers says what ONE waits for and gives a partner or a lawyer "Seguir sem essas respostas" (`close_account_gaps`: the client is asked no more, ONE creates the lawsuits, the piece leaves those points to a lawyer); the client's "Terminei" says what they still owe of the account instead of "o escritório prepara a sua ação"; with no CPF lookup in the environment, the person shows no "CPF não consultado". The owner's test case sat with the piece never asked while the firm saved a person twice to "validate" it.
- **A paper a client signs is asked where it is owed** (phase 3, 2026-11-09, migration 0123): a bill in somebody else's name the firm accepts says under it, on the case's card of documents, who the holder is and who they are to the client, that ONE read the name and may have erred, and where the client's declaration of residence stands (the client's word, an address of another city a person looks at, ONE issuing it, the client signing it, signed); the client is asked on their link only who the holder is, and the declaration then comes to sign below; going on without it is a partner's or a lawyer's, with the second factor, a reason and what it may cost confirmed first. A name ONE read in capitals and the database wrote as a name is the same name: the line says a name was corrected only when its words changed, never its case or spacing.
- **The case says what happens now, and follows ONE by itself** (the owner's audit of the case screen, 2026-11-05, migration 0096): the top of `/casos/[id]` ("Andamento do caso", `journey-card.tsx` over `caseNow` of `lib/cases/now.ts`) says what ONE is doing at this moment, what holds the case and whose turn each thing is (the firm with the function that acts, the client, ONE, the platform of ONE), and what ONE did a moment ago, each line one click from its card; who has the case is computed from those lines, and every wait's owner comes from one table (`WAIT_PLACE` of `lib/cases/advance.ts`), which the center of what waits reads too. The case and `/inicio` follow the database by themselves (`useLiveRead` over `case_pulse` and `firm_pulse`), saying when they last knew they were current; the spinner turns only while ONE works. Each card says on its head whether something there asks the firm, waits for the client, failed or is being worked on by ONE, opens when something comes to ask the firm there and closes once it is done, unless the person opened it. "Mudar etapa" is the exception, in the header's quick actions, saying what each stage switches off. The owner felt lost on the case: it said "Com o ONE" while the package waited for the lawyer, and nothing changed on the open screen until a reload.
- **One action at a time on the case, said where the person acted** (the owner's canvas "ONE · Ações no caso", approved 2026-11-10): a write a card of `/casos/[id]` starts goes through `useCaseAction(section).run` (`casos/[id]/components/case-action.tsx`, kept by `useCaseActions` in `case-view.tsx`): its button turns and says the verb, every other control of every card waits disabled with why on pointing (`CollapsibleGroup` `lock`, a fieldset per body), reading stays free (a card opens and closes, a file opens: `component="span"`), and the lock lifts only once the case read again is on screen. What came of it is a green line with the time at the top of that card (`notes`), a card it changed is ringed for a moment (`flash`) and one "Ver" away in a toast, a failure stays in its card. "Andamento do caso" says the action under way with a bar and dims what may change, says "Atualizando o caso" while a change made by ONE or the client is read again (nothing locked), changes at the same moment as the cards and marks what changed with "Novo" (`useFresh`). A dialog that saved on its own (`onChanged`) locks the same way until the case shows it, unless the person is typing in a field. The owner found a click in one card leaving the others live, nothing said while it ran or when it ended, and the Andamento stale for seconds after the cards changed.
- **A card's list that piles up is one line per thing, what asks first** (the owner's canvas "ONE · Publicações no caso", approved 2026-11-10): Publicações, Documentos, Avisos ao cliente and Assinaturas of `/casos/[id]` list through `ItemRows` (`components/product/item-rows.tsx`): "Pedem a sua ação" first, each line saying what is missing in the attention colour with its one button (the awareness of ONE's triage given in place; a triage, a document to decide, a paper to confirm opened in the line by its button), then ONE's work as lines that wait, then what is settled with what came of it, the newest first, a few in sight and "Mostrar mais". A line opens in place on everything it holds, one at a time (a publication as `PublicationCard` `inline`, the document with its buttons), and what opened once stays mounted. The card's chip counts what asks, and arriving by the top's "Resolver" opens the first line that asks. Where each thing stands is a pure rule (`lib/publications/rows.ts`, `lib/cases/document-rows.ts`). Prazos puts what waits for confirmation or a second look first, and Resultado lists the facts that ask (confirm ONE's reading, the awareness, the value received) above the lawsuits. `/publicacoes`, a list worked in volume, keeps its open cards. The owner had to read every publication to find the one that asked for the lawyer's awareness.
- **An alarm is what a person can put right** (the owner, 2026-11-05): the home counted every registration whose DJEN reading was late, under "Atrasado", and in an environment that does not read the DJEN every one is late for good, with nothing to do on `/publicacoes`. The firm's home and center count of the reading only a reading that stopped for a lawyer still on record and a communication left out, under "O ONE precisa de você"; a late reading is ONE's own, said on `/publicacoes` as "o ONE tenta de novo sozinho", and where the DJEN is not read the page says only that.
- **`/publicacoes` reads in the order of the work, and ONE's latest work on a case is a row of cards** (the owner, 2026-11-05): the reading of the DJEN is one `CollapsibleCard` at the top (`#leitura`), then the filters, the list's head with its search, "O que cada resposta quer dizer" one click away, and each publication as `PublicationCard` (`components/publications/publication-card.tsx`, shared with the case): what it is with its icon and chip, its facts, the court's words on a tint, the six answers, and at its foot the case, to whom, "Calcular prazo" or the hearing where the triage says so, and the rest in `QuickActions`. "O que o ONE fez há pouco" on the case's top is `ActivityCards`, the same kind of event one card with its count; since 2026-11-06 (the owner found the top "muito carregado") each card is compact and the whole card leads to its card on the case, and "O que trava o caso" is each line's icon, sentence, whose turn in words under it and its one button, with no count chips nor frames.
- **Each decision where it belongs** (the owner, 2026-11-05): an answer of the AI that waits for a person is decided on its case (the piece in "Peças", a document in "Documentos", the order of the facts in "O que aconteceu") or in its conversation, and the home and the center of what waits lead to it one line each (`lib/ai/decisions.ts`); `/aprovacoes` is out of the main menu, a list of every decision for whoever reviews many, reached from the center's "Ver todas as decisões", and never holds the text of a piece.
- **The passenger is interviewed one question at a time** (the owner, 2026-11-04, migration 0095): what the piece needs of what happened is asked on WhatsApp right after the yes, one question per message from the firm's script summarised (`@flyee/rules/interview`; never the purchase, which the piece takes only from a document), what ONE knows left out with why on the case, and the same questions one per screen on the client's link; the card "O que aconteceu" shows each answer, lets the attendance answer for the client and a lawyer go on without the rest.
- **The client's account reaches the piece, whenever it comes** (the owner, 2026-11-05, migration 0097): the card "O que aconteceu" waits for the client's answers, with "Seguir sem essas respostas", and the top of the case lists them under "O que trava o caso", only before the lawsuits; once they exist the card says the answers hold nothing and go to the piece, and the top says nothing of them. ONE writes again by itself a piece the AI wrote before the account changed, while it still has a `[COMPLETAR]` and no lawyer touched it (`advance_redo_pieces`, `draft_redone` in the timeline). The owner's case looked stalled: the card said ONE created the lawsuits after the answers when they already existed, and the piece written the night before never read the account.
- **A piece asks a lawyer only for what its claim cannot stand without** (the owner, 2026-11-05, migration 0099): the piece writes `[COMPLETAR]` only for an essential fact (the flight, the arrival, a bag's fate: data by kind of problem), leaves any other fact out or writes it as the client said it, and says each in its notes; what it still lacks ONE asks the client, one question at a time, and writes the piece again with the answers; the top of the case says the piece waits for the client meanwhile. The owner's redone piece had six `[COMPLETAR]` a lawyer had to fill. Questions are asked only of a piece written after the client's latest answer (0100), never of one ONE is about to write again, and the card "Peças" says "O ONE pergunta ao cliente" or "O ONE escreve de novo" instead of "Para revisar" while ONE carries the text; the card "O que aconteceu" keeps the answers of every round in sight.
- **ONE files the lawsuit itself** (the owner, 2026-11-05, migration 0101): where the court's own machine channel (the MNI) is open to ONE, or for a test case in the test court, the card "Protocolo" says "O ONE protocola" and where it stands (about to go, going, tried again at a time, filed with the protocol and that ONE may err, refused with the reason, failed, sent with no answer), and the lawyer's own steps step back; they come back, as the contingency, where the court gives ONE no channel or ONE's filing came back. A filing that may have reached the court is never sent again until a lawyer says the court's system does not have it.
- **What ONE's automation needs of a person is asked when the work starts** (the owner, 2026-11-05, migration 0102): ONE's signature in the cloud needs a lawyer's authorization that lasts seven days at most, and it was asked only once the package was ready, so ONE stopped there. It is asked from the moment ONE creates the lawsuit, in the center of what waits, on the team's card "Assinatura em nuvem" (which makes "Renovar a autorização" its main button before it ends, the last provider kept) and on the lawsuit in the card "Protocolo"; the CPF it needs is asked in the setup of who signs. A filing that came back (refused, failed, sent with no answer, taken with no number) is a line of the center too, never only a line of its case.
- **A state that may have changed is not said as unchanged** (the owner's test, 2026-11-05, migration 0103): the top of a case said "A audiência ainda não foi marcada" while the publication of the court that marked it waited for triage. With no hearing set, a publication that waits is said as what may be the hearing, with what follows its triage (ONE registers the hearing and tells the client); and a test case's client is told of a hearing the test court marked minutes ahead at once.
- **ONE triages, the lawyer confirms being aware, on the case** (the owner, 2026-11-05, migration 0104): an automation that acts on what it classified (a publication's triage, which records the hearing and counts the deadline) is applied at once with a person's awareness asked after it, not held for a person's word before it: the card says it was ONE (that ONE may err) and offers "Estou ciente" and the way to correct it, the center of what waits asks until it is given (red after the setting's hours) and the bell repeats it. Everything of a case is worked on the case: its publications have their card there, and the firm-wide list (`/publicacoes`) keeps what has no case, the reading's health and the volume.
- **"Com o escritório" is of the trip that became a case, never of the passenger** (the owner, 2026-11-05, migration 0106): the agency's screens said it of the person and locked every card, so a passenger who travelled again could not be asked to agree to the next trip, its documents could not be sent and ONE never followed it. The trip's chip says "Caso no escritório" and only that trip loses its actions; the record, the documents, the next trips and the link stay open, each document that went with the case says so, and the list and the agency's home read one rule (`passengerStanding`): an agreement owed again first, "Com o escritório" only with nothing ahead, "Caso no escritório" beside a next trip.
- **Every registration opens in the side panel, and "Adicionar" is at the top** (the screen pattern after ADVBox, approved by the owner on 2026-11-07): a new deadline and a new hearing, and their corrections, open in `SidePanel` (`DeadlinePanel`, `HearingPanel`) from their cards on the case, `/prazos`, `/agenda` and a publication; the "Adicionar" of the top (`components/layout/add/`) offers, by group, what this person may add (today "Casos": Novo prazo and Nova audiência, to who signs or runs the deadlines), asks first for the case and then the lawsuit the court has, and says once saved what now holds with the way to the case. The finance's entries and "Nova pessoa" join it with their phases; it is not shown on the setups nor to a partner's people.
