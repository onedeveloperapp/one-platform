# Product (authenticated app) UX

- The `product-screen` skill is MANDATORY for any create/edit here — a `PreToolUse` hook (`.claude/hooks/product-screen-guard.mjs`) loads it automatically; you never wait to be asked.
- **Goal, not table**: design from the user's job and what success looks like. Never default to a CRUD table of rows — that shape is allowed only when the job really is scan/compare/manage many rows.
- **Never a blank screen**: every zero-data view uses `EmptyState` (icon + why + the ONE next action). A brand-new user must find a path to value (the aha moment).
- **Less text on the screens, and no empty space** (the owner, 2026-11-09): a page, a card or a section opens on its title, and what it is for sits behind the "i" beside it (`description` of `PageHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CollapsibleCard` and `SectionTitle`; 200 characters at most, kept by `lib/i18n/hint-budget.test.ts`). `detail` keeps in sight only state or data (the kind of a case, a conversation's number, how many a list shows) and what must be read before acting (leaving the firm, what each decision of `/settings/autonomia` does, what a lawyer authorizes with the cloud signature, the release of a real passenger's alerts, and the instructions on the client's and the passenger's own pages). A row or a column that opens a list shows a chevron, never a sentence saying so; a definition that repeats its title goes; a tile is its icon and its name. A section with nothing in it takes a drawing of `@/icons/illustrations` (`EmptyState` `illustration`): the court with no lawsuit, the board with no case going, the goals with none, a module with no case, and every list with nothing in it (the cases, the conversations, the publications, the deadlines, the agenda, the filing queue, the library, the approvals, the operator's consoles); a state of permission or of a failed read keeps its icon. The owner found the controladoria, a module's area and the library tiring to read: 136 blocks of text under titles, about 2,800 words. The case screen had the same, card by card (2026-11-10): each card with nothing in it takes its drawing beside its words (the dossier's, in the narrow column, above them), a section inside a card says what it is behind the "i" of its sub-heading (`SectionTitle` `variant="subtitle2"` `as="h3"`: the suspension of the Tema 1.417, the acceptance of the civil court, the audios of the case, the preview of a piece), the way out of ONE's automation (a petition written outside, the reason a family goes together) sits behind an `InfoHint` beside its state line, and a state line drops the sentence that repeats the button beside it.
- **Onboarding never diverts attention**: a setup or onboarding screen (`/configurar`, the setup on the home, any `SetupWizard`) carries nothing that leads away from finishing it: no link to another screen, no "see more", no list of things to explore. Each step does its work in place (dialogs, inline forms, the second factor in the step); what is information for later lives on the home or its own page. A line of modules with "Ver módulos" on the first step of `/configurar` took the owner out of a setup half done (2026-10-30). The header's search and "Adicionar" are left out while the firm's or the person's setup is on screen, on `/inicio` too (`useMarkSetupShown`): there the browser filled the search with the owner's e-mail, and its "Nada encontrado." covered the panel of the papers (2026-11-21).
- **"Continuar" saves the step** (the owner, 2026-11-24, on the canvas "Configuração do escritório, antes e depois"): in every `SetupWizard` the primary saves all the step holds and goes on, or stays and says what is missing beside the field; nothing in a step is called "Salvar"; only that button is filled, what acts at once (sending a document, connecting, inviting) is outlined and says its outcome in its line, and a decision is the primary's own words ("Aprovar e continuar", "Publicar 1 roteiro e continuar", "Pedir ao ONE"). What is typed lives in the flow (`useOfficeDraft`, the operation's answers), "Voltar" and the list of steps lose nothing, a forward click in the list and "Continuar depois" save what is complete (`onLeave`). Every step has its `label`, listed beside the card. The address had its own "Salvar" over "Continuar" and was lost by whoever went on; a script stayed a draft under a filled "Publicar"; the power of attorney and the address were asked in "O escritório" and again in "O que o cliente assina", now one step; "O ONE deve preparar os pacotes de protocolo?" came after the step that asked how to file, and now opens it (0285 leaves the filing out of the home's list for a firm that said no); "recebe clientes de agências" repeated a channel and is read from it.
- **Prerequisites come first**: in a setup or any multi-step flow, whatever a later step cannot be done without (the second factor, a role, a connection, a fact the database checks) is its own step before the first step that needs it, and the flow does not advance past it until it is met. Never let a person reach a step and find it locked by something an earlier step could have settled. The second factor is the first step of `/configurar` for this reason (2026-11-03): it was asked inside six steps, and blocked the owner at the first.
- **ONE does the work, a person answers** (the owner, 2026-11-03): before a screen has a person do a task by hand, ask whether ONE's AI can do it from what the firm already has (read a document, fill a record from it, make a model of it, check it against a rule, ask for what is missing), and build that instead. The screen shows what ONE did and that ONE may err, and asks only what only the person knows or decides; a document ONE cannot use is refused with why and the way to send another. How much ONE does alone is each firm's (`autonomy_decisions`, ONE by default). The setup's step of the papers had no way to send the firm's own power of attorney: the firm sends the document it already uses, and ONE makes the model (migration 0079). The model keeps the firm's words (0093): only what changes per case becomes a mark, each field of the firm's form its own (`[[NOME]]`, `[[CPF]]`, `[[ENDERECO]]`), and the firm's society and lawyers stay as written once it says they are its own; the case says when a lawyer who signs is not among them.
- **ONE's work is never the person's task** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a checklist, a count, a pending line, an empty state or a message names as the person's only what a person does or decides. What ONE does by itself (opens a case from a module, sends a link, creates the lawsuits, writes a piece) is said as ONE's work, done, being done or waited for ("O ONE espera o primeiro caso"), never as a step the person owes, and a manual way to do it is offered as the exception it is, never as the primary action. "Abrir o primeiro caso" on the firm's setup list made a firm that had just finished its setup feel the work was manual, the opposite of what ONE is sold on.
- **A registration starts from documents** (the owner, 2026-11-03): where the record is already written on a document the person has (an identity, a proof of address, a ticket, a booking's screenshot, a photo of a printed page), the form opens on sending it; ONE reads it, fills the fields and marks each as read, and the person confirms or corrects what ONE read, being asked by hand only what no document gives. Typing every field is the contingency, offered as "Preencher à mão", never the first screen. The agency's new passenger was a form of typed fields while ONE already read the identity, the proof of address and the ticket on the passenger's page; it now starts from them (migration 0083). A document sent for one job fills every record it carries: the power of attorney and the contract the firm sends for its models fill "Dados do escritório" (the address, the e-mail, the society's registration, the CNPJ, the lawyers) where it has nothing, marked "Lido pelo ONE" until a person confirms (0271); the setup had asked by hand for the address both papers wrote.
- **A card asks only for what is still owed** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a request already fulfilled (a document that arrived and serves, a link already sent, an answer given) leaves the card's main area. The card says it as done (`StateLine` `done`, a green check; when nothing is left in the card, one line on its tint that says so: "Não precisa enviar de novo") and keeps doing it again one click away, in `QuickActions`. What is still owed, or arrived and does not serve, is its own line in the attention colour with its one button beside it. The passenger's link showed "Enviar identidade" to a passenger whose agency had already sent it. A receipt ONE read is not attached yet: the protocol's foot says "O recibo ainda não está anexado" in the attention colour, with "Confirmar e anexar" in that line. The owner sent the test case's receipt four times and stopped at "Lido", the button below repeating the word of the upload (2026-11-24).
- **Where things stand is said in icon and colour**: every card a person works through leads with the icon of what it is (`CardTitle`, its family's tint) and says where it stands in a `StatusChip`; each thing listed in it says its state in a `StateLine`: done in green with a check, under way in amber with a clock, to look at or act on in accent-3 with an exclamation, a failure in red (kept for failures), information in grey. The words always say the state too, never the colour alone.
- **An act the person must understand is confirmed in front of them**: what binds them or what others will act on (an agreement given or taken back, a decision) is confirmed in an `OutcomeDialog`: the state in a large icon, the title in their words ("Acordo registrado"), each consequence on its own line with its icon, one button ("Entendi"). A line of `Alert` at the top of the page is read past: the passenger who agreed on their link could not tell what they had agreed to start.
- The record of each screen (what it shows, its files and migrations, and what the owner found behind it) is in `apps/web/ARCHITECTURE.md`, under its area. Read it before changing a screen, and write the new entry there in the same change; the rules those entries gave are below.
- **The case says what happens now, and one action runs at a time.** The top of `/casos/[id]` says what ONE is doing, what holds the case and whose turn each thing is; every wait's owner comes from one table (`WAIT_PLACE` of `lib/cases/advance.ts`). The case and `/inicio` follow the database by themselves (`useLiveRead`). A write a card starts goes through `useCaseAction(section).run`: the other controls wait disabled with why, reading stays free, the lock lifts once the case is read again, and what came of it is said in that card. When it moved the case's trail, the screen goes to "Andamento do caso", which marks itself (`showTrail`), and what came of it stays one "Ver" away. A card's list that piles up is `ItemRows`: what asks first, then ONE's work, then what is settled, each line's standing a pure rule. While ONE works on a record its screen waits under `AtWorkNotice`, for ONE's own work only. A panel open on ONE's work draws `AtWorkBody` in place: the model ONE makes from a document shows the ring, its four steps (the sending first), "Enviado às", "Desistir", "Fechar" at its foot, and where the model waits once closed ("Esperam você" or the setup's step); the step of the papers says the request in one `StateLine` whose mark turns while ONE works (2026-11-21, after a still list of steps left the owner unsure anything was loading).
- **One rule per fact.** Who has a case is `case_standing` on every screen, and a count never comes from another rule. `/casos` is the only page that lists the cases, and a count opens a list of the size it says. Two views of one menu never share a name. "Com o escritório" is of the trip that became a case, never of the passenger (`passengerStanding`).
- **Each setting has one home** (the owner, 2026-11-11). "Sua conta" is the person's (Perfil, Segurança, Atuação profissional); "Escritório" is the firm's (Dados do escritório, Equipe, Regras dos casos, O que o ONE decide, Mensagens ao cliente, Conexões, Plano e pagamentos, Apagar dados de teste); each contracted module has its group (`firmSettings` of its definition). "O que o ONE decide" holds only ONE's decisions, by the stage of the case; the agenda, `/publicacoes` and the setups say how a setting is and lead to its home or use its component.
- **ONE acts, and the person is told.** An automation that acts on what it classified is applied at once and asks the person's awareness after it ("Estou ciente"), with the way to correct it. What ONE's work needs of a person is asked when the work starts. A wait on the client always has a way forward for a partner or a lawyer. A state that may have changed is never said as unchanged. An alarm counts only what a person can put right. Each decision is made where it belongs, on its case or in its conversation, and `/aprovacoes` never holds the text of a piece.
- **What a piece asks.** The client is interviewed one question at a time. A piece writes `[COMPLETAR]` only for a fact its claim cannot stand without, and ONE asks the client the rest. A name ONE read is said corrected only when its words changed.
- **The firm's hard lines.** A filing that may have reached the court is never sent again until a lawyer says the court's system lacks it. ONE never charges a client, never tells a client of a judgment by itself, and nothing on a screen says ONE pays. A fact ONE read is corrected with its reason, never deleted. What ONE did is counted for the firm, never per person. The fees and the books show only to who reads the books; a client's money is never revenue; a line that may repeat another is said, never dropped; a number never averages fewer than five lawsuits.
- **People's data.** A CPF in a list shows only its middle. Refusing marketing never stops the notices of a lawsuit, and a segment never holds a minor nor who refused marketing. Exporting or erasing a person's data, and taking the whole portfolio, are for who decides for the firm, with the second factor; "Levar a carteira inteira" is a button in plain sight, never in a menu. What leaves ONE (an e-mail, a calendar link) names the act and the case's number, never a typed title.
- **Every registration is managed the same way.** The list with `ListBar` and its "Novo" (none on a list of what ONE makes; making one by hand stays in "Mais ações"); creating and editing in `SidePanel`, the document first where there is one; what another record uses is archived with its reason, and only what nothing uses is deleted, in a `ConfirmActionDialog`; every change audited, and the finance's registrations ask the second factor. "Adicionar" at the top offers what this person may add, never on a setup nor to a partner's people.
- **Lists, numbers and exports.** A list's view and order live in the address, so a tile opens the view it counts; a search goes in the request. A page of numbers only shows: each number opens `/casos` with the page's cut, it says whether it counts test cases, and what ONE does not count yet is said so, never drawn as zero. A test case says "Teste" wherever it is listed. An export saves nothing of a list it could not read whole and says on its first page what it is. A board ONE moves never offers to drag a card. A package something holds is "Com impedimento", never "Pronto para protocolar", by one rule on the case, on /protocolo and for ONE's own filing (`filing_package_hold`, 0288): the card had learned that a package built for a lawsuit that moved, or lacking the e-mail the eproc asks, is held, while the queue still said it ready and offered it to a batch.
- **The home lays out by what it holds** (the owner, 2026-11-12): under the greeting, what holds a case in the wide column, what to follow (`CountList`) and ONE's line of the month in the side one, a column with nothing in it not drawn and the other then across; the portfolio's card across below. A line, a list of one and a tile of one each across `/inicio` left most of it empty.
- **Cards in a row line up** (the owner, 2026-11-12): the controladoria's board has its columns after the plans of a pricing page, sharing their rows, each part of the way named over its columns in one colour, an empty column saying "Nenhum caso" in its shape; "Com quem está" and "Tribunal" are two cards of one height with the same head. The court's numbers had been a heading over seven cards beside a card.
- **Who owes what, where, with what** (the owner, 2026-11-12, on the people's card of the case): a card's head says one state (`ask` of `CollapsibleCard` with the screen's state, never a chip beside it); what the client owes says who answers it and since when, from their link (`client-asked.tsx`), with "Preencher pelo cliente" or "Enviar pelo cliente" as the exception; a fix that lives on another card leads there (`#case-people`); "Confira" comes with the document, both values or the source in front; facts under their names (`FactGrid`); "Depende da plataforma" for what the platform owes, nothing of the environment. The leg of the trip's problem is ONE's to mark (0228), and the card asks a person only when the record cannot tell.
- **Pessoas laid out by what filters what** (the owner, 2026-11-12): the funnel's cards right over the list they filter, each with its share in a ring (`NumberCard` `ratio`); the numbers of who the firm serves after the list, as `TallyTile`s four across; a trend drawn only where a series exists.
- **A record inside a card reads in one order** (the owner, 2026-11-13, on a lawsuit of "Processos"): head (name, one state, its edit and "⋯"), facts filling their rows with ONE's reasons behind each fact's "i" (`FactGrid` `hint`), what is owed as one line each with its answers beside it, then its foot; never a box with its own chip inside the record.
- **The case's cards read in that order too** (the owner, 2026-11-24, on the canvas "Cartões do caso, antes e depois": the chronology a block of dates, "Como este caso vai a juízo" with no order to follow, "Peças" with fields, buttons and loose texts, "Protocolo" the most mixed): each card says first what is owed, one line each with its button (the qualification still open, the cause of the delay not recorded, the e-mail the court's screen asks, the petition ONE approved alone with "Ler a petição", a package to build again), then its facts in a `FactGrid` (the qualification, the priority, what each claimant asks, the Tema 1.417), then its content, then its foot. A value with its own "Salvar" is a fact with "Definir" or "Mudar" that opens a `SidePanel` (the Valor da Causa Pedido, the triage of the Tema 1.417, another piece); "Descartar", "Escrever outra peça", "Montar de novo" and "Cancelar pacote" go in the record's "⋯"; an account's chronology is a `ChronologyTimeline`; the ficha of the protocol opens one step at a time, a step that lacks a value of the case saying which; a package the court cannot take yet (a contact its screen asks, built for a lawsuit that moved, held by its case, its files still to sign) says "Com impedimento".
- **The library says what waits and what ONE writes with** (the owner, 2026-11-13): "Esperam você" first (what ONE asks, a model ready to check, a refusal, a citation with no source, one button each), then "Com que modelo o ONE escreve" (a tile per use: the petition of each kind of lawsuit with its models by problem, each paper and declaration, the pieces of the process), then the list; "Novo modelo" opens on the upload, what ONE read marked "Lido pelo ONE", "Modelo padrão" ticked and said when the firm has none of its own for that use; ONE compares the firm's data in a document with its record and never asks what matches (0244).
- **The client's page follows what the client does** (the owner, 2026-11-13): one rule (`clientStanding`) gives the head's count, the numbered steps in the order of the case and "Já feito"; each section in one frame (`ClientSection`), its own card or its content inside a step; what the firm asks of a hearing beside the hearing; the lawsuit in one card; nothing says "ONE" to the client.
- **The forecast is the case's, at a glance, and never a promise** (the owner, 2026-11-14): one rule (`market_forecast_of`) on every screen; the case shows it open under its top (chance as a ring, the value as a range, how it ends, each level beside it), the next hearing what the airline does there and where an offer falls, `/numeros` the sum and how it came out; the base and the sample always said, "pode errar" behind the "i"; never on the client's page, never to choose a judge.
- **A card's actions in one place each** (the owner, 2026-11-12, from "Incluir trechos" beside "+ Incluir pessoa"): adding to a card's list is `add` of `CollapsibleCard` ("+ Incluir ..." at the foot), the list elsewhere is `more` ("Ver ... ›" at the foot), the top keeps only what acts on the whole record; an item's edit at its head, its removal in its "⋯"; adding says "Incluir".
- **Shape.** Every route of the logged area has a `loading.tsx` drawing `PageSkeleton` in its form, and a new page gets its own. A state on a menu item floats over it (`CornerTag` `edge`), and a submenu closes once a page is reached. There is no menu of the modules (the owner, 2026-11-14): a module's work is the pages under it, its overview is in the firm's settings, and the home names the modules in one line.
- **Core and module setups are apart** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a setup asks what every firm needs in the core (`/configurar`) and what one module needs in that module's own setup, in the module's colour, after the core and before the home, only for the modules the firm contracted; the others are shown disabled, as what the firm could add. Never put a module's parameter in the core's setup, nor ask a firm to set up a module it does not have.
- **Everybody who joins has a setup of their own**: a firm's member, an agency's colleague; the second factor first, then what their function needs (a lawyer's registration at the bar), never a home with nothing said.
- Long setups use `SetupWizard` (progressive disclosure); steps to first result use `OnboardingChecklist` + `@flyee/onboarding` (state only; step definitions live in the project's code).
- **Engagement**: completion drive (`ActivationProgress`) and competence feedback only. Never ship points, badges, leaderboards or streaks — documented failures, and streaks carry regulatory risk.
- MUI v9 + theme tokens only, next-intl for every string, inline form errors near the field, skeletons that reserve space.
- **Semantic fields**: phone, CPF/CNPJ, CEP and money never go in a raw `TextField` — use `components/product/fields/*` (mask + check-digit validation + CEP auto-fill via `@flyee/fields`); persist `onlyDigits()`, never the masked string.
