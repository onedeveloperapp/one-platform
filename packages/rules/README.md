# @flyee/rules

The pure rules of ONE's domain (engineering rule 1 of `CLAUDE.md`: rules are code). No I/O, no clock of its own, no framework: a rule takes what it decides on, the day included, and answers. The AI never decides a rule; it may only suggest what a rule then checks.

The database holds the same checks where a write needs them (the CNJ check digits in `cnj_is_valid`, the CPF in `cpf_is_valid`, a flight number in `apply_segment_fields`, migration 0022), so a screen and the database agree.

## What is here

| Export | What it decides |
|---|---|
| `@flyee/rules/cnj` | The unique case number of the Brazilian courts (Resolução CNJ 65/2008, Annex VIII, read on 2026-10-09): `parseCnj`, `isValidCnj`, `formatCnj` (`NNNNNNN-DD.AAAA.J.TR.OOOO`), `cnjCheckDigits` and `composeCnj` (modulo 97, with BigInt). Which court a `TR` names is data, the `courts` table of migration 0021 |
| `@flyee/rules/flight` | An airline designator and an airport code, a flight number read and normalized (`G3 01234` is `G31234`), a booking reference, and `arrivalDelayMinutes`: the difference of two local times of the same airport, so no time zone is needed |
| `@flyee/rules/oab` | A registration at the bar: the section's state and the number as that section writes it |
| `@flyee/rules/person` | A day (`YYYY-MM-DD`) read strictly, a person's age on a day, whether they are a minor on it, and the day as people write it (`dd/mm/aaaa`) |
| `@flyee/rules/forum` | `decideForum` (`foro-v1`): the passenger's domicile by default (CDC, art. 101, I; Lei 9.099/1995, art. 4, III); any other forum only in a city of an airport of the trip and with a recorded reason. Cities compared without accents or case; which comarca a city belongs to is the court's data |
| `@flyee/rules/proceedings` | `planProceedings` (`tipo-de-processo-v2`): a minor can only claim in the civil court (Lei 9.099/1995, art. 8), and so can an adult whose own claim exceeds forty minimum wages, the ceiling counted per claimant and never for the sum (art. 3, I; STJ, Jurisprudência em Teses, ed. 89, tese 2). When a minor is among the claimants, the whole family goes to the civil court in one lawsuit (`together`, the default: the TJMT and a chamber of the TJDFT took the split for abusive fragmentation in 2026); the split, the adults in the small-claims court and the minors in the civil court, is a lawyer's recorded choice (`split`, planned as the first version did). An adult above the ceiling goes to the civil court alone, the rest of an adult family staying in the small-claims court. The answer says how the family stands (`single`, `together`, `split`). The minimum wage, the forty and the choice are data |
| `@flyee/rules/weather` | `readMetar` and `weatherSigns`: what a METAR observed (visibility, the lowest broken or overcast layer, the weather groups, gusts), never its trend or remarks, and the signs in it under thresholds the caller hands in. A sign is a reason to look, not a conclusion |
| `@flyee/rules/suspension` | `triageSuspension` (`tema-1417-v1`): the risk that the national suspension of Tema 1.417 of the STF (ARE 1.560.244) reaches a case, from the incident, what the airline told the passenger and the weather signs: a cause of the CBA, art. 256, par. 3 (weather, airport infrastructure, an authority's order, a pandemic) is high; a cause of the airline itself is low; baggage and a downgrade are out of it. A risk for the firm to weigh, with its grounds named: whether a lawsuit is suspended is the court's |
| `@flyee/rules/state-count` | `countCausesByState` (`inscricao-suplementar-v1`): a lawyer's causes per state in a year against their registrations at the bar; more than five in a state without one there is habitual practice and calls for a supplementary registration (Lei 8.906/1994, art. 10, par. 2; Provimento CFOAB 178/2017) |
| `@flyee/rules/deadlines` | `computeDeadline` (`prazo-v1`): the day a procedural term set in days runs out, over the court's calendar the caller hands in. Business days only, unless the term is in calendar days (CPC, art. 219; Lei 9.099/1995, art. 12-A); the day it runs from left out and the last counted (art. 224); a communication of the DJEN published on the first business day after it was made available and counted from the business day after that (§§ 2º and 3º; Resolução CNJ 455/2022, art. 11, § 3º, as Resolução CNJ 569/2024 wrote it, from 16 May 2025 on); no term in business days runs from 20 December to 20 January, in every court, the small-claims courts too (art. 220; Resolução CNJ 244/2016, art. 3º), nor on a day the court suspended the terms; a last day of partial business moves to the next business day (art. 224, § 1º). The national holidays of federal law (Lei 662/1949, Lei 6.802/1980, Lei 14.759/2023) are the rule's; the rest of a court's calendar (its days without business, its suspensions, its days of partial business, by kind of lawsuit, system and comarca) is data. Conservative: what it cannot know is counted as a business day, which never puts a deadline later than it is, and said as an assumption (a year whose calendar nobody checked, a comarca not known or whose own holidays are not on record, a day of the calendar for a kind or a system not known, a partial day at the start, a start day that is no business day, a publication before the DJEN's rule, and a term in calendar days that ran through a suspension of the procedural terms, which the rule cannot tell procedural: it does not stop there, which gives the earlier day, and says that a procedural one ends later). Every day that did not count is named, with why, and the count says when each year of the calendar it went through was checked (`checkedYears`). A person confirms every deadline |
| `@flyee/rules/flight-problem` | `flightProblem` (`flight-problem-v2`): whether a trip ONE follows has a problem to tell its passenger, from what a source says of each leg in its airports' local times, the legs taken in the order they fly and cut into journeys where a stay of more than a day or another airport starts the next (`journeysOf`: a round trip is two): a leg cancelled, then a leg the airline changed, then the last leg of a journey landing late at its destination by the setting's minutes (four hours, where Resolução 400 of ANAC, art. 21, gives another flight, the refund or another way to travel); a connection lost shows as the later arrival. `assistanceDue`: what the airline owes for it (art. 27: from one hour the means to communicate, from two food, from four lodging when a night is spent; the choice of another flight, the refund or another way to travel for a cancellation, a delay of four hours and a change the passenger does not accept, arts. 12 and 21). The rule names what is owed; the words are the app's |

`npm test -w @flyee/rules` runs `src/rules.test.ts`, `src/domain.test.ts` (the cases of the domain as tables: domicile, the place of the fact with a reason, a minor, a family, an external cause), `src/deadlines.test.ts` (the deadlines over the calendar of TJGO for 2026: Carnival and its partial Ash Wednesday, the year's end, a publication during the suspension, a day of one comarca, a day of one kind of lawsuit or one system, calendar days, what the rule cannot know) and `src/flight-problem.test.ts` (a cancellation before a change and a change before a delay, a delay under the setting told nothing, a connection lost, a round trip's way out or way back landing late, a stay and another airport ending a journey, what the airline owes by the hours waited) on `node --experimental-strip-types`, like the adapters (`packages/tsconfig.adapters.json` typechecks it).

## What joins, and when

- M4: prescription (two and five years) over the same calendars.

Each rule comes with its version and its tests; what changes a rule is a new version, never an edit that rewrites what an old case was decided by.
