# ONE · platform monorepo

ONE is a SaaS for mass-litigation law firms in Brazil, built in this repository. Many firms share one database from day one (one organization is one firm, isolated by RLS). The first product module covers air-passenger claims against airlines (delays, cancellations, overbooking, baggage) for passengers from all over Brazil. The development plan is `docs/ONE-PLANO-DESENVOLVIMENTO.md` (milestones M0 to M6, approved on 2026-10-08). This repository was copied from the flyee.club platform on 2026-09-30; M0 removed the code that only served that product. The product document is `docs/PRODUCT.md` (rewritten for ONE on 2026-10-22); the other docs, except the plan, still describe flyee.club and are historical.

## Safety

- `apps/web/.env.local` is a blank ONE file. Fill a key only with an ONE development or sandbox credential. The files that pointed at other products are in `C:\AppsProjects\_quarentena-env-one`: never copy them back into the repo.
- Run `db:plan:remote`, `db:migrate:remote`, `db:gate:remote` or `test:db:remote` only while `.env.local` points at an ONE Supabase project (since 2026-10-21, the development one). That project has the baseline, so every change to the database is a new migration file: never edit a migration that a hosted project applied.
- Never commit `.env` or `.env.local`. Commit per milestone; never push without being asked.
- **Finish the work you were given.** Carry each task to its end, and diagnose an ordinary obstacle (an error, a timeout, a locked file, a failing tool) and work through it. Stop only where these rules name a stop: the one step a person must take, a secret, a decision that is the owner's, and what a rule protects. Confirmation before a risky or irreversible action still holds. Close with a recap that stands on its own: what you found and what you did.
- Never store a lawyer's password, 2FA seed or digital certificate (A1, A3 or cloud) anywhere: not in the database, the vault, logs or fixtures. The vault keeps a vendor's API token and nothing else; it refuses the names, and a catalog test refuses such a column. The six digits a WhatsApp number that lives on the platform alone is registered with are typed by the firm, used once and kept nowhere. The owner decided on 2026-10-14 that ONE stores what an automation of its own needs of a lawyer's credentials (the filing in a court's system). Its custody is built for one thing so far, under its own rules (migration `0041_cloud_signatures`): the authorization a lawyer gives ONE at the provider of their certificate in the cloud (a PSC), which is a token of a signature session and never the certificate, its key or the factors that unlock it, sealed in the vault as a connection of that lawyer with the address it was issued at, given only by the lawyer for themself with the second factor and for a certificate of the CPF they declared, living at most 7 days, opened only for a use declared first and bound to what it says (the very files of a package of a lawsuit they sign, under the backend's claim) and kept on record, what it signed leaving ONE only in that lawyer's hands, and ended at once by the lawyer, a partner, who manages the firm, the lawyer leaving, the PSC, or the platform moving the PSC's address. A password, a 2FA seed or a certificate's file stays as written above until a custody of its own is built for an automation that needs it, and never through the PSC's optional `pwd_authorize`, which collects the holder's factors.
- `WHATSAPP_SIMULATOR=1` is for a development machine or a preview: production never honours it, a simulated number never reaches the vendor, and a delivery signed by the simulator can only touch simulated numbers. `META_APP_SECRET` is what authenticates every webhook delivery: never commit it.
- `VAULT_KEYS` seals the firms' vendor tokens. Never commit one and never share one between environments. A key that is lost takes every token it sealed with it: the firms reconnect their accounts.
- Fixtures hold invented people only: never a real person's CPF, name or case number. A lawsuit's number in a fixture is of a year to come (2099, with its check digits), so no real lawsuit carries it; a CPF is one every manual uses as an example (123.456.789-09, 529.982.247-25, 111.444.777-35).

## Product decisions the code assumes

- **What needs no person, ONE's own AI does** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a task that asks no decision of a person and that ONE's AI can do from what the firm already has (read a document, fill a record from it, make a model of it, check it against a rule, ask for what is missing) is ONE's work, sparing the firm time, people and cost. A person is asked only what only they know or decide (an answer, a choice of the firm's, a document to send again after a refusal said with why), each firm says how much ONE does alone (`autonomy_decisions`, ONE by default), and what ONE did says that ONE may err. Before suggesting or building a feature, ask first whether ONE can do the task instead of the person, and what it would need to ask; a screen where a person does by hand what ONE could do is the exception, with its reason.
- **ONE's work is never shown as the firm's task** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a checklist, a count, a pending line, an empty state or a message names as the firm's only what a person of the firm does or decides; what ONE does by itself (the case opened at a passenger's yes or from an agency's passenger, the client's link sent, the lawsuits created, the piece written, the package asked for) is said as ONE's work, done, being done, or waited for. A step "Abrir o primeiro caso" on the firm's setup list told a firm that had just finished its setup that the work was manual, against what ONE is sold on: the first case arrives through a module the firm contracted and ONE opens it, so the list ends at what the firm gives ONE (who signs, the team, the channel) and the home says ONE waits for the first case. Opening a case by hand stays, as the exception it is.
- Many firms from day one, with roles inside a firm (sócio, advogado, controladoria, atendimento). A person belongs to one firm at a time (decided by the owner on 2026-10-09; the database enforces it). Passengers never get platform accounts: they reach ONE through links by token.
- A travel agency may be a firm's partner in the passenger module (decided by the owner on 2026-10-29): an account of its own, never a firm's, in partnership with one firm per module at a time. It registers the passengers it covers in an area of its own; the firm reads nothing of them until a passenger says yes to its word about a problem, and the agency nothing of the firm.
- ONE is sold by a technology company with no lawyer partners. It is software for firms: it never acts as a law firm.
- A module of ONE is a package a firm contracts with its subscription (decided by the owner on 2026-10-30): Direito do Passageiro is the first; Direito Previdenciário and Direito Trabalhista are shown as coming soon, and no firm can have them until they are out. Each module the firm contracted has an area of its own with its lawsuits, the main menu lists the modules, and the pages of the work read every module or one (the owner, the same day).
- What a partner decides for the firm (who signs, the Valor da Causa Pedido, what ONE decides alone, the words of the clause its agencies register) is decided, in a firm where nobody is a partner, by who manages the firm account.
- The firm's setup is its core, what every firm needs; what one module needs is that module's own setup, in the module's colour, after the core and before the home, for each module the firm contracted, the others shown disabled; and each person who joins a firm has a setup of their own, the second factor first (the owner, 2026-11-03).
- The module: air-passenger claims. Adults go to the small-claims court (juizado especial cível); a family with a minor goes to the civil court (vara cível) together, in one lawsuit (decided by the owner on 2026-10-14); splitting it (the adults in the small-claims court, the minor in the civil court, linked lawsuits of one case) is a lawyer's recorded choice. An adult above the small-claims ceiling goes to the civil court alone. Each claimant of a lawsuit in the civil court accepts it before the filing. The forum defaults to the passenger's domicile; another forum needs a link to the flight and a recorded reason.
- The notices about a client's own lawsuit go on WhatsApp with no agreement asked; the client stops them on their link, by asking the firm, or with SAIR (decided by the owner on 2026-11-03).
- The Valor da Causa Pedido (what each claimant asks, by kind of problem) is the firm's to give or not: with none, the lawsuit goes with no value defined and the judge defines it (decided by the owner on 2026-10-27).
- Assisted filing: ONE builds the filing package per court system (Projudi, eproc, PJe, e-SAJ); the lawyer files in the court system with their own login. Every certificate type works because signing happens there; where the court's system takes each file already signed (the Projudi of TJGO, a `.p7s`), ONE signs the package's files with the cloud certificate of a lawyer of the lawsuit who authorized it (since 2026-10-15), as the firm lets it.
- ONE never moves case money and never charges the passenger. It bills only its own subscription (Asaas, BRL). The line of the money (the owner, 2026-11-07, the plan of the controladoria and the finance, decision 1): ONE records, reads, calculates, prepares and warns of a firm's money and its clients'; it never holds, receives, passes on, splits, pays or charges money, not even on a firm's behalf (no account, sub-account, commission, charge, reminder or payment link of a client's), and the code of the firm's books never reaches ONE's own billing (a rule of the lint, `apps/web/eslint.config.ts`).
- WhatsApp through the official Cloud API, with each firm's own number, in coexistence with the phone app.
- The firm's brand on everything a passenger sees. pt-BR only.
- **Decided by the owner on 2026-10-14** (the three decisions the points of 2026-10-13 depended on): what ONE decides alone reaches the lawyer's own acts too (the text of a piece, the decision to file or hold, a deadline), by a parametrization each firm changes, with automation first; a lawyer's certificate, password or second factor is stored when an automation of ONE needs it (the filing in a court's system: the custody and the channel are designed from the notes of 2026-10-14 on the MNI, the courts' systems and the cloud certificates, kept out of the repository); and a family with a minor goes to the civil court together (built, the fifth part of S10).
- **Decided by the owner on 2026-10-15** (on the filing by ONE in a court's own channel, after the research of 2026-10-14): build first what needs no court's authorization (the lawyer's cloud signature through a PSC, the signed file a court takes, the MNI client, each with a stand-in where the vendor or the court stands); never build an automation a court considers fraud, but only where that is certain (a written rule of the court or the CNJ that forbids it); where it is only a forecast, the direct automation (ONE in the court system's own screen, with the lawyer's credentials in custody) may be built. Each court is checked against its written rules before its screen is automated.
- **Decided by the owner on 2026-10-27** (on the travel agency linked to a firm): the passenger's documents an agency attaches come into ONE and ONE uses them, the passenger's agreement given in the agency's contract with them; this replaces "no data passed" of 2026-10-08. Not built yet: how an agency enters ONE (a link of the firm's, an account, an integration with the agency's system) is the owner's to choose, and the legal opinion on soliciting clients (already waited for) answers whether a firm may receive clients so, since the Estatuto da Advocacia (art. 34, IV) forbids taking causes with or without a third party, whatever the client agreed to; the agency's clause has to be a specific consent, highlighted among the others (LGPD, art. 7, § 5, and art. 8, §§ 1 and 4), with a parent's specific consent for a child's data (art. 14, § 1). What an agency attaches is then read as any document (above).
- **Decided by the owner on 2026-11-05** (the audit of the case screen): the case and `/inicio` follow ONE by themselves; a card opens when something comes to ask the firm there and closes once it is done, and its head says where it stands; whether a decision is the judgment is asked only when its words do not say it; the 72-hour notice only warns while the platform is tested, and becomes a block before production (not opening the case, or waiting for a lawyer to release it: to be decided).

## Engineering rules

1. **Rules are code.** Forum, kind of proceeding, deadlines, prescription, eligibility, the count of cases per state and the Tema 1.417 suspension are pure, versioned, tested functions. AI never decides a rule.
2. **AI suggests and records; the firm says how much ONE decides alone.** Every AI run is stored (task, prompt version, model, effort, input hash, output, cost). Since the owner's decision of 2026-10-14, ONE decides by itself by default and each firm chooses, per decision, to have a person check a share or decide every one (`autonomy_decisions`, `firm_autonomy`); the operator may hold a decision for every firm when its measure falls. A rule (forum, deadline, kind of proceeding) is still code, never the AI's.
3. **Every vendor sits behind an adapter** with a real implementation, a fake and a contract test with recorded responses. No coding task waits for an external key, account or approval. A new adapter follows the seven that exist: sources import their siblings through the package's own exports (`@flyee/courts/types`), tests import with the `.ts` extension, only erasable TypeScript (the contract tests run on `node --experimental-strip-types`), and the package enters `packages/tsconfig.adapters.json` and the root `test:adapters` script.
4. **Configuration is data:** plans, limits, the court-systems table, file limits, forensic calendars, API versions and AI prices live in tables, never in constants.
5. **The firm is on everything:** RLS on every table, explicit grants in every migration, RPCs that receive the firm, the service-role client never handed to an AI tool, and every new pgTAP test runs with two firms.
6. **Jobs are idempotent:** claim-then-send (`for update skip locked`), what is due recomputed from the data on each sweep, never `step.sleepUntil`. The work a person waits on is done right after their answer (`soon`, `apps/web/src/lib/jobs/soon.ts`), never through the job runner; everything else is a sweep listed in `apps/web/src/lib/jobs/registry.ts`, run by the one clock (`one-tick`) and, when it serves what people wait on, while ONE is used. No event is sent to the runner.
7. **Privacy as code:** consent per purpose with frozen text, retention jobs, export and deletion per person, no personal data in logs or error messages, no tracker inside the logged area or the client links.
8. Every mutation records an audit event (`recordAudit` in `apps/web/src/lib/audit.ts`).
9. Visual identity changes ONLY in `packages/design-tokens/css/*.css`; run `npm run tokens:generate` and commit `tokens.generated.ts` with it. The palette is locked to `theme-blue` (provisional) in `apps/web/src/constants.ts`, `apps/web/src/config.ts` and `apps/web/src/style/global.css`. The palette, the logo and the type change only when the owner asks.
10. The logo is the single component `apps/web/src/components/logo/logo.tsx`, reading the provisional ONE art in `apps/web/public/brand/one-*.svg`; every raster is derived by `npm run brand:assets -w @flyee/web`. When the official logo arrives, replace those four SVGs and rerun the script.
11. npm workspaces: install dependencies from the root (`npm install`), never inside an app. The workspace scope stays `@flyee/*` for now.
12. Icons: Phosphor through the tsconfig alias `@/icons/nexture/*` → `src/icons/phosphor/*`.
13. The dark band (footer, deep sections) is `bg-background-deep`. Red is reserved for risk and failure.
14. Copy for people (site, app, e-mails, WhatsApp): pt-BR, plain; no dash (— or –), no exclamation mark, no emoji. Enforced by `apps/web/src/lib/i18n/copy-rules.test.ts`. The operator console is exempt.
15. One language. `LOCALES` (`apps/web/src/constants.ts`) is a list of one and every per-language table is a `Record` over it; a second language is a data change the compiler walks through, never a fork of the code.
16. **No component that needs a paid licence** (the owner's decision of 2026-10-21, as in the MedChina project): MUI X's community packages (MIT) only, and what only a paid tier gives is built in the project's own code. The lint refuses `@mui/x-*-pro`, `@mui/x-*-premium` and `@mui/x-license`, and no licence key exists.

## Platform decisions

- Deploy: Vercel, only through GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) with the Vercel CLI and a token, as in MedChina (since 2026-10-21): a push to `main` deploys production, a pull request a preview, and a manual run production unless preview is chosen. A production deploy then syncs the app with Inngest (a `PUT` to `/api/inngest` at the production address, the repository variable `PRODUCTION_URL` or the current one): Inngest runs only the functions of the last sync, which since 2026-11-04 is one, the clock `one-tick` (on 2026-10-24, before it, ONE never answered in the test chat until the app was synced by hand). The Vercel project is `one-platform` of the team ONE (the Vercel account `onedeveloperapp`), never connected to GitHub on the Vercel side, with its root at `apps/web`, the files outside it included, Node 22 and its functions in São Paulo (`gru1`); its production address is `one-platform-two.vercel.app`, public and indexable (each deployment's own address sits behind Vercel's login). The first deploy, of `f4e0c2a`, went up on 2026-10-21 with no database: the public site, served from `gru1`. The owner then set on Production the Supabase keys (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, `DATABASE_URL`), `VAULT_KEYS` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; `WHATSAPP_SIMULATOR`, `CALENDAR_FAKE` and `PSC_FAKE` are set there too, and production honours none of them (they act on a preview). The repository's secrets are `VERCEL_TOKEN`, `VERCEL_ORG_ID` and `VERCEL_PROJECT_ID`. Every commit is authored as `onedeveloperapp <onedeveloper.app@gmail.com>`, the owner of the Vercel account, set in this repository's own git config (MedChina's commits are `flayeelab`'s the same way): on the Hobby plan Vercel builds only a commit whose author is the team's owner, and blocks any other (`TEAM_ACCESS_REQUIRED`) while the workflow waits for it. On this machine the Vercel CLI's own login is another account's: the ONE account is used as `vercel -Q C:\Users\tribo\.vercel-one <command>`, and `.vercel/project.json` (git-ignored) links the repository to the project. Database, auth and storage: Supabase (Postgres + RLS). ORM: Drizzle. Email: Resend. Jobs: one clock at Inngest, on its free plan (`one-tick`, every five minutes), and the work a person waits on right after their answer (2026-11-04). Billing of the ONE subscription: Asaas (BRL).
